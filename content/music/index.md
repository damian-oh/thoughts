I studied music, wanted to leave it, and eventually started playing again. These notes concern listening, making music, and the distance between understanding how something works and knowing what to do.

1. [[Keeping It Human]]: An examination of AI, provenance, and what makes music worth listening to.
2. [[I Thought I Was Done With Music]]: A reflection on leaving music and returning to it.
3. [[What Noel Gallagher Knew]]: The limits of technical understanding and the knowledge behind artistic choices.
4. [[Wrong Notes and Backspace]]: A preference for revision and the unexpected enjoyment of improvisation.
5. [[Not Yet]]: A new sense of possibility in NELL's music after years of listening.
6. [[A Logic Project]]: An account of discovering unexpected production methods behind music I had already admired.
7. [[The Second Jam]]: A late-night jam in Hongdae and what happened when I back inside.
8. [[Triple Function]]: Exploring harmonic analysis and asking what could justify it.
