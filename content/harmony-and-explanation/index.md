An ongoing investigation into how the suitability of pitches and pitch collections can be explained within harmonic contexts. These notes document the questions, assumptions, explanations, and tests that emerge as I investigate the problem with AI. The aim is not to establish a theory in advance, but to examine what different explanations can and cannot justify.

1. [[The Question Before the Scale]]: How a question about tritone substitutions led to a more fundamental investigation.
2. [[What Does Suitable Mean?]]: Comparing C Lydian and C Locrian over `Cmaj7` and examining the idea of melodic freedom.
