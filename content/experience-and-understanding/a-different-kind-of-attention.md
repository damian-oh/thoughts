I remember an exam from a C# .NET MAUI course at Niagara College.

At the time, I was also working remotely for a company in South Korea. Another developer had left unexpectedly, leaving me with a considerable amount of work. I had slept for only about an hour the night before the exam.

The exam required us to implement various features, including images, animations, navigation, and event handling. When it began, I read through the requirements one by one and started writing the code. I worked through the tasks without much difficulty and finished the exam.

Afterwards, the professor approached me.

"I was watching you the whole time, and that was pure beauty."

I remember those words. But I do not remember experiencing an unusually high level of concentration during the exam. I had simply read the requirements and implemented what they asked for.

I have experienced concentration quite differently in other activities.

When I used to [[Under the Bar|squat heavy weights]], I did not have to make myself concentrate. I could approach the bar without thinking, and the moment the weight settled on my shoulders, an extreme level of concentration took over. There was a heavy barbell on my back, and I had to lower myself under its weight and stand up again. At heavy weights, there was little room for anything else.

I did not have to remind myself to pay attention. The weight demanded it. As soon as I returned the bar to the rack, that intense concentration disappeared. It simply happened when the weight was on my shoulders.

Playing guitar has been different.

In May 2026, I attended a [[The Second Jam|jam session at Strange Fruit]] in Hongdae, South Korea. During my first performance, I struggled to follow the music. When I returned to play again, I made a conscious effort to concentrate. This time, I was much more satisfied with my playing. Unlike the squat, the guitar had not demanded my attention simply because I picked it up.

I think about these experiences together sometimes.

I remember the moment concentration took over under a heavy barbell. I remember deciding to concentrate at the jam.

But I cannot identify a similar moment during the programming exam. I simply started with the first requirement.
